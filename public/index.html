App Guide & Troubleshooting

🔒 Are my email credentials safe?

Absolutely. This app runs entirely on an isolated container in your AWS EC2 private virtual machine. Your email address and App Password are securely encrypted at-rest using AES-256 Symmetric Encryption before hitting MongoDB, and are only used to connect directly to your email provider's secure IMAP server.

📧 Gmail Connection setup

For security, Google does not allow external apps to login with your normal Gmail password. You must use an App Password:

  1. Go to your Google Account Settings.
  2. Search for or select 2-Step Verification and enable it if not already done.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select App passwords.
  4. Enter a custom name (e.g. "Job Tracker") and click Create.
  5. Copy the 16-character code and paste it as the password in this app.

⚙️ Other providers host info

  • Outlook/Hotmail: Host: outlook.office365.com, Port: 993
  • Yahoo Mail: Host: imap.mail.yahoo.com, Port: 993 (Requires an app password)
